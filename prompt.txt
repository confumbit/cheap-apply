Do not assume an email pattern is correct merely because it is common.

## Email Verification

For every candidate email, classify it as one of:

### VERIFIED

There is a public source explicitly displaying the complete email address and connecting it to the person.

### STRONGLY CORROBORATED

The complete address is not publicly displayed in one source, but multiple independent sources establish:

* the person's identity and company;
* the company's email domain;
* the company's email-format convention; and
* the resulting address is consistent with that convention.

### INFERRED

The address is constructed from a likely company naming convention but there is insufficient independent evidence.

Never present an inferred email as verified.

If possible, check additional public evidence such as:

* previously published emails
* company documents
* conference bios
* GitHub commits/profiles
* press releases
* cached/indexed pages
* reputable email-verification services

**Do not claim an email is "working" merely because the format is valid.** A mailbox can only be described as confirmed working if there is legitimate evidence that the mailbox accepts mail. Do not send test emails unless explicitly authorized.

## Important Rules

* Use only **publicly available professional information**.
* Do not expose personal/private email addresses when a professional address is appropriate.
* Do not guess a hiring manager's identity based solely on their job title.
* Do not manufacture email addresses.
* Do not treat email-format guessing as verification.
* Cross-check identities carefully where multiple people have similar names.
* Prefer current employment information over stale information.
* Pay attention to the **specific business unit, geography, and department**.
* If the job posting contains a requisition ID, search that ID directly.
* If the posting identifies an external ATS, investigate the ATS/company career page as well.
* Search for people who have recently posted, shared, or commented about the role.
* If no individual email can be established, provide the best legitimate recruiting/company contact instead.
